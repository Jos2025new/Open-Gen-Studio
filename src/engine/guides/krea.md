Krea 2 (turbo / medium / large) — image prompting guide (optional)
Source: open-generation-studio/skills/prompting/krea.md, adapted to this app.

- Photoreal with a low "AI look"; cheap.
- Concise natural language, written like a photo brief: subject + setting + composition + lighting + mood + medium ("35 mm, overcast soft light, eye level"). Order is weight: what comes first counts most. Under ~50 words is enough on turbo.
- Style comes from reference images (1–5); keep the prompt about content (who, where, doing what).
- Text: one short quoted line at most. No negative field: state the positive ("clean studio sweep").
- Weak at several subjects with relationships and at identity across many images (use a top model).
