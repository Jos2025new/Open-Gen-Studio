Sketch, clay model or floor plan → photoreal architectural render

1. What the input fixes
- The attached image is the design: massing, proportions, roof shape, openings (count, size and position), floor levels and the camera viewpoint stay exactly as drawn. The model adds materials, light, landscape and context, never new volumes or windows.
- Look at the input and list what you will keep before writing: "two-storey box with a cantilevered upper floor on the right, five vertical windows, flat roof".

2. Use it as an edit, not a new image
- Image step with the sketch in refs (an edit route), prompt in the edit pattern: "Turn this sketch into a photoreal architectural render. Keep the exact massing, proportions, openings and viewpoint. Add only: <materials>, <light>, <landscape>."
- Hand sketch: clean straight lines, real materials. Clay / white model: keep every edge and shadow line, map materials per surface. Floor plan: either a top-down rendered plan (materials and furniture on the drawn layout) or, when asked for a perspective, describe the room from the plan and say it is an interpretation.

3. Materials and light
- Name the material for each surface (facade, frames, roof, ground, interior floor and walls) and one light condition (see skill:archviz).

4. Next steps
- Approved render → more views with the same building (skill:archviz) or a walkthrough (skill:archviz-motion).

5. Avoid
- Extra floors, moved or added windows, changed roof, perspective different from the drawing, cartoon or sketchy leftovers unless asked.
