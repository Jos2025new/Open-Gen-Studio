Grok Imagine Image (xAI) — image prompting guide (optional)
Sources: docs.x.ai image generation; Higgsfield, as an industry reference. Adapted to this app. (Grok Imagine Video has its own guide, grok.)

- Write a short scene in natural language, not a list of tags: subject → action → setting → light → style, in one or two sentences.
- Name a concrete mood ("melancholic", "tense", "quietly triumphant") instead of generic adjectives such as "beautiful" or "amazing".
- Give the light a source and direction ("low sun from the left, long shadows") and the style a medium ("35 mm film photograph", "gouache illustration").
- Text in the image: short, in "double quotes", with its position.
- Edits: start with what must stay as it was (identity, pose, framing, background), then state the single change. One change per call.
- Several references: say what each one contributes. The number of references (xAI documents 5, other platforms 3) and how the prompt cites them come from the endpoint's schema; use exactly the syntax the model's inputs list, and do not invent one.
- Avoid stacked edits, negations ("no people") — describe the empty scene instead — and keyword piles.
