P-Image (Pruna) — image prompting guide (optional)
Source: open-generation-studio/skills/prompting/p-image.md, adapted to this app.

- Very fast and cheap: moodboards, drafts, final upscales.
- Atmosphere + one focal element, with the medium named ("stylized digital illustration", "documentary portrait, 50 mm"). One or two sentences: long prompts get averaged.
- Edit: one change with a precise verb (replace, remove, recolour, add) and an explicit keep list — "change only the jacket to red; keep face, pose, lighting and composition identical".
- Upscale: the last step; any prompt only about sharpness and clean-up, never new content.
- Avoid text, several related subjects and identity across many images (use a top model). State positives, no negatives.
