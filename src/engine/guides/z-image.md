Z-Image Turbo / Base (Tongyi) — image prompting guide (optional)
Source: open-generation-studio/skills/prompting/z-image.md, adapted to this app.

- Fast and cheap; realistic photography lands best.
- Long narrative prose (80–250 words): shot and subject → age and appearance → clothing → environment → lighting → mood → style or medium → technical → constraints stated as facts. Name textures and atmosphere ("worn denim, salt-stained", "humid dusk haze").
- No negative prompts (ignored): put exclusions as facts at the end ("the street is empty, the sky is clear").
- Image-to-image: describe the transformation, not the source; a sketch or rough render works as a composition scaffold.
- Weak text: avoid it or keep one short quoted word. Weak at multi-subject relationships.
