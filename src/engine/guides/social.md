Social ad — router and criteria (this guide decides; it generates nothing itself: each piece goes to one production flow)

1. Brief (one questions card, only what is missing; never re-ask what the card answered)
- Objective: awareness, consideration or conversion. Platform and placement (Reels/TikTok 9:16, feed 4:5 or 1:1, YouTube 16:9, display). Market and language.
- Creator on camera, voice-over only, or product only. Target duration. Offer/CTA: only a real one the user gives.
- Text on screen: yes or no (see 5). The concept: offer 3 options (see 2) with your recommended one as default.

2. Concepts (3–6 when asked; 3 as card options by default)
- Each: hook (visual + first spoken line + sound cue, in the first 2 seconds), beats in order, audio treatment (on-camera voice, voice-over, music only), production format (see 6), CTA, creator needed or product only, whether it depends on on-screen text.
- Say plainly they are ideas: there is no market research behind them (no ad-library or trend data in this app).
- Only when the user brings a reference ad (image or video frame): keep its mechanism (rhythm, structure, the hooking gesture) and move topic and payoff to the user's product. Never a copy.

3. Claims (hard rule, applies to every step of every flow)
- Use only benefits the user stated or that are legible on the product. No invented price, stock, shipping, discounts, results, metrics, comparisons or testimonials. No verified claim → neutral copy.
- Urgency only with a real offer or date.

4. Copy: three layers, never mixed
- Scaffold (in the plan): hook line, beats, CTA and the claims used, written in each step's title and prompt.
- Final ad copy (in your text message that goes with the plan, as a paste-ready block): each field within its limit. Meta: primary text ~125 chars before "see more", headline ~40. TikTok caption ~100. YouTube title ≤100. Google Ads: headlines ≤30, descriptions ≤90. Variants by angle, not synonyms: problem–solution, transformation, social proof (only if real), curiosity, objection reversal, identity.
- On-screen text (hook burned in, step labels, cards): decided before generating and written into the generation prompt, only if the user said yes. Keep it short and quoted literally.

5. Route: one format → one flow (a choice, never two for the same piece)
- UGC with a creator (review, unboxing, try-on, tutorial): workflow:ugc with the chosen variant.
- Static ad pack (images): workflow:social-set; one master → other ratios.
- Same approved master to other ratios (1:1, 4:5, 9:16, 16:9): reframe (and upscale if needed) on the approved piece; never regenerate it.
- Cinematic product piece: 2+ clips → workflow:shot-sequence; one shot → workflow:image-to-video.
- Edit an existing video (background, clothing, object, restyle): the video_edit op; longer → video_extend.
- Motion / typographic / product-only: key image, then animate it (typography stays short; see 4).
- Two formats asked (e.g. unboxing and tutorial) = two pieces, each its own flow; never a hybrid. One creator and one product per video, as @Name subjects.

6. Before spending
- If the material does not fit the format (no product photo for a UGC try-on, a video too long to edit), say so and offer what fits before proposing the plan.
- Default social format 9:16 at medium quality (never a fixed 1080p). If the requested duration is not allowed by the model, say the allowed values when proposing; never change it silently.
- Each image, video and audio step costs credits: the plan card shows the price and nothing runs until the user approves.

7. Vertical layout and timing (9:16, 1080×1920)
- Safe zone: keep faces, product and any on-screen text out of the bottom ~320 px (caption, buttons) and the top ~220 px (account name, progress bar), and ~60 px from each side; the right edge carries the action buttons on Reels and TikTok. Write it into the prompt as composition ("product centered in the upper middle third, clear space at the bottom").
- Hook in the first 1–2 seconds: the first frame already shows the payoff, the product or a surprising action; no logo intro, no slow fade-in.
- Length by placement: TikTok and Reels 9–30 s (15 s is a safe default); Stories ≤15 s per card; YouTube Shorts ≤60 s; feed video 6–15 s; bumper 6 s. Longer only when the user asks.
- Loop-friendly endings for Reels/TikTok: the last beat can lead back to the first.
