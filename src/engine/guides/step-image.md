Step Image Edit 2 (StepFun) — image prompting guide (optional)
Source: open-generation-studio/skills/prompting/step-image.md, adapted to this app.

- Fast instruction-based edits and text-to-image; good at pose, clothing, background, text and visual reasoning.
- Prompts up to ~512 characters (longer is cut).
- Edit: one direct instruction in plain words plus what stays — "Make the cat lie on its back showing its belly. Keep the face, lighting and framing." One change per call.
- Text-to-image: one photographic description with subject, setting and one light cue.
- The negative prompt is mostly ignored: write positives.
